# Stations and Facilities

> Sample document for the Whoosh AI prototype. Not an official operator document. Actual facilities can differ at each station.

## Common facilities

Whoosh stations generally provide waiting areas, toilets, prayer rooms, customer service, and journey support facilities such as lifts or elevators where available. Availability depends on the station.

## Accessibility

Passengers who need accessibility assistance should contact official customer service or station staff before travelling. This prototype has no real-time facility data.

## Asking for facility locations

- Check the signs in the station area.
- Ask station staff or customer service.
- Follow the information boards and announcements.

## Facilities that are not working

Report broken facilities to station staff. You can also create a simulated report through the Complaint Assistant in this app with the station_facility category.
