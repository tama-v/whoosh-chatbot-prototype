# Drafting a Complaint (Demo Document)

> Sample document for the Whoosh AI prototype. Reports in this prototype are an internal simulation and not official operator tickets.

## Helpful information

- Problem category: lost_and_found, ticket_issue, boarding_issue, station_facility, train_service, or other.
- Time and date of the incident.
- Location, station, or train number when relevant.
- A short chronology with facts, not guesses.
- Contact information that can be used to follow up the report.

## Complaint Assistant flow

1. Describe the problem in chat or in the form.
2. Heiser helps pick the category and points out missing information.
3. Review the report summary.
4. Confirm to create a simulated report number.
5. Track the report status on the Track Report page.

## Simulated statuses

The available statuses are submitted_to_demo_system, under_review, resolved, and closed. These only describe the demo flow inside this app and are not connected to operator systems.
