# Tickets, Changes, and Cancellations (Demo Document)

> Sample document for the Whoosh AI prototype. Not an official operator document and not a ticketing system. All transactions must use official channels.

## Changing a ticket schedule

Schedule changes must be made through the official channel where the ticket was bought. Seat availability, change fees, and deadlines follow the ticket conditions that apply.

General steps:

1. Open the official operator ticket sales channel.
2. Enter the ticket details and passenger identity.
3. Choose an available replacement schedule.
4. Check the change fee before confirming.
5. Save the latest ticket proof.

## Cancellations and refunds

Ticket cancellations are made through official channels. Refund rules, deductions, and payout timing follow the ticket policy that applies. This prototype cannot process cancellations or refunds.

## Buying or booking tickets

Whoosh AI does not book, pay for, or issue tickets. Use official operator channels for every transaction.

## If a ticket cannot be found

Contact official customer service with the passenger name, booking code, and payment proof. Do not send sensitive data through unofficial channels.
