# Whoosh Boarding Guide

> Sample document for the Whoosh AI prototype. Not an official operator document. Always verify with official operator channels.

## Before leaving for the station

- Make sure the passenger details on the ticket are correct.
- Prepare the identity document and ticket proof that staff may check.
- Check the departure station, train number, and schedule on the ticket.
- Arrive early so you have time for checks and for reaching the gate.

## Boarding process

1. Enter the station area and follow the security checks.
2. Show your identity and ticket proof to staff when asked.
3. Go to the waiting area or gate shown on the ticket and information boards.
4. Follow boarding announcements and staff directions at the gate.
5. Take all belongings before leaving the waiting area.

## If the gate or schedule changes

Gate and schedule changes may be announced at the station. Check the information boards, listen to announcements, or ask staff. This application is not connected to operator real-time schedule systems.

## If you miss the train or arrive late

Contact station staff or the official customer service channel immediately. Change or replacement rules follow the ticket conditions that apply, and this prototype cannot confirm them.
